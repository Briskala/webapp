# Briskala on Kickstarter — Campaign Copy (English)

## Project Title

**Briskala — Clear accounting & financial management for everyone**

## Tagline

*Bookkeeping without spreadsheet stress — from solo to SMB*

## Subtitle

The platform for invoices, expenses, VAT, and reports — with built-in multi-company accounting.

---

## The Problem

Accounting is too expensive, too complex, or too fragmented:

- **Freelancers** pay hundreds per year for tools that are overkill.
- **SMBs** juggle Excel and separate apps — no single view of cash flow, VAT, and outstanding invoices.
- **Accountants** waste time exporting/importing between systems.

Most solutions are US-centric, expensive, or built for enterprise — not for European small business.

---

## The Solution: Briskala

**Briskala** is a complete accounting and financial management platform:

- ✅ **Invoices** — create, send, track to payment
- ✅ **Expenses & receipts** — register and categorize
- ✅ **Bookkeeping** — double-entry ledger, automated postings
- ✅ **VAT & tax** — filing-ready overviews
- ✅ **Reports** — P&L, cash flow, KPIs
- ✅ **Multi-company** — multiple administrations, roles & permissions
- ✅ **Secure** — session-based access, company isolation, audit trail

---

## What's Already Built?

A **working MVP** — not mockups:

- Marketing site (features, pricing, FAQ)
- Integrated accounting dashboard
- Company picker with secure URLs
- Modules: invoices, expenses, bills, contacts, payments, chart of accounts, VAT, reports
- Demo mode for instant testing
- Double-entry postings to append-only ledger

*See demo video and screenshots in this campaign.*
