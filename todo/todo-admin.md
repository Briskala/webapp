Website rules / business positioning

Briskala is not an advisory company.

Briskala is a financing company where users can apply for financing for:

Car financing
Real estate financing
Housing construction & consultation

Briskala is also an insurance company.

Users can apply for and take out insurance through the website, such as:

Car insurance
Boat insurance
Liability insurance
General insurance applications

The website should clearly focus on financing applications and insurance applications, not on general advice services.


Briskala employee workflow

Briskala employees must be able to manage all applications in the admin panel.

Employees can:

Review and process customer applications
Approve or reject applications
Mark applications as “in progress”
Request additional documents from the customer
Upload the insurance policy or financing documents
Link uploaded documents to the correct customer number
Send updates/messages to the customer

When an employee requests additional documents, the customer should see this request in their account. The customer must be able to upload the requested documents directly through the customer portal.

When a policy or document is uploaded by an employee, it should only be visible to the correct customer based on their Customer No., for example: BPAG-402345.
